# Vacine-se

An Android app developed with **React Native** during the COVID-19 pandemic, designed for local businesses and centered on a rewards program for vaccinated customers.

## About the Project

**Vacine-se** was created to involve local businesses in an initiative to encourage vaccination.

The idea was to enable participating businesses to offer rewards to vaccinated customers visiting their premises, combining a community-focused initiative with a customer engagement strategy.

## Objectives

- Encourage vaccination through a rewards program.
- Help local businesses build relationships with their customers.
- Encourage local businesses to participate in initiatives with a positive social impact.

## Target Audience

- **Business owners:** businesses interested in offering rewards to vaccinated customers.
- **Vaccinated customers:** people visiting participating businesses who were eligible for rewards.

## How the Program Worked

The program connected visits by vaccinated customers to participating businesses with rewards offered by those businesses.

Vaccination status was verified in person by presenting a **vaccination card at the entrance to the establishment**.

Staff checked the card on site, and participation in the rewards program depended on this verification.

## Technology

- **React Native:** the framework used to develop the Android app.

## Development Context

Created during the COVID-19 pandemic, the project explored how a mobile application could address a need at the time by combining vaccination incentives with engagement between local businesses and their customers.
